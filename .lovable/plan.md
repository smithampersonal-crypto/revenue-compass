## 3. Content outline (about 500–600 words)
A. **ARC — Ayden's Revenue Compass** (intro): ASC 606 workpaper application taking a contract from review and judgment through performance obligations, transaction price, allocation, revenue schedule, contract balances and journal entries. Supports, does not replace, the accountant.

B. **How ARC works** — three short labeled paragraphs:
- AI interprets contract evidence — reads an uploaded contract, drafts facts and points to the supporting passages.
- Deterministic engines calculate results — allocation, schedules, balances and entries come from fixed application logic, never generated by the model; same inputs, same outputs.
- The accountant owns the judgment — every AI draft is reviewable and editable; open items need explicit resolution before an analysis is finalized.

C. **Accounting judgment, software discipline**: why ARC emphasizes source evidence and traceability, validation, explicit review, stopping (not guessing) when evidence is insufficient, and preserving accountant control. Principles only.

D. **A portfolio project**: built to demonstrate technical accounting and ASC 606 application, product design, AI-assisted workflow design, testing/validation and auditability. No biography, résumé claims or contact details.

E. **Scope**: a project/workpaper application; not a commercial accounting system and not a substitute for professional accounting judgment. Does not post to a ledger, bill customers or produce financial statements. One short paragraph.

Representative copy tone: "ARC separates what a contract says from what the accounting requires. AI helps read the contract; the calculations are performed by deterministic logic; the conclusions belong to the accountant."

Excluded: test counts, package/phase history, schema/prompt versions, model name, quotas, infrastructure, vendors, dependency versions, security internals, hype words.
